How it works

  1. Create your group

    Sign up, name your group and choose your game day.

  2. Invite your friends

    Share a link on WhatsApp. An admin approves each person who asks to join.

  3. Vote each week

    Everyone says in or out, so you can see who's coming at a glance.

  4. Make the teams

    An admin gets two balanced teams in one tap, then shares them with the group.

Why the teams come out even

Balanced by position
Each team gets a keeper where possible, and defenders, midfielders and forwards are split evenly.
Balanced by skill, on the paid plan
Players rate each other from 1 to 5. Nobody in the app can see who gave a score, and the ratings even out the teams.
No app to install
It's a website made for your phone, so anyone in your group can use it from a link.
